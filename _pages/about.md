I am an AI researcher and machine learning engineer (Ph.D., Computer Science and Engineering, University of Nevada, Reno) working on **distributed learning, foundation models (LLMs), and mathematical optimization**. My work spans **healthcare AI**, where I build privacy-preserving, wearable-based methods for tracking early-stage cognitive decline, and **energy and infrastructure AI**, where I apply federated learning to power grids, EV ecosystems, and data centers.

Before moving into AI, I spent nearly a decade as a control, power, and data center engineer, designing and operating critical infrastructure (electrical distribution, UPS, diesel generators, cooling, DCIM/BMS). That background lets me build models that work on real sensors, real constraints, and real operations.

## Selected results

- **80% less training time** for federated and split learning on heterogeneous clients (IEEE Internet of Things Journal, 2024).
- **35% lower communication and storage** than LoRA for fine-tuning ViT, LLaMA, and GPT-2 with subspace reparameterization (SOLAR, under review).
- **26% less slower-side compute** in decentralized learning with convergence analysis (IEEE ICDCS, 2024).
- **88&ndash;90% accuracy** on 3,877 power grid events across 23 PMUs with a privacy-preserving federated framework using homomorphic encryption.
- **22% lower energy consumption** and **18% less downtime** in data center infrastructure projects; led a team of 32 and supervised three data center builds totaling $35M.

## Two tracks

**AI for Healthcare.** Multimodal wearable data (PPG, EDA, EEG, IMU) to extract physiological biomarkers and distinguish Mild Cognitive Impairment from healthy cohorts; cloud pipelines on AWS SageMaker; clinical decision-support with LLMs. See my [academic CV]({{ '/assets/pdf/Mahmoud_Sajjadi_CV_Academia_Healthcare.pdf' | relative_url }}).

**AI for Energy &amp; Data Centers.** Federated fault detection, PMU event classification, EV smart charging and V2G, energy prediction and control for data center cooling and power. See my [industry CV]({{ '/assets/pdf/Mahmoud_Sajjadi_CV_Industry_Energy.pdf' | relative_url }}).
