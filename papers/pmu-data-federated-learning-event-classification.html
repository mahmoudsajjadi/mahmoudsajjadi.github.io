Towards Distributed Learning of PMU Data: A Federated Learning Based Event Classification Approach

Seyed Mahmoud Sajjadi Mohammadabadi, Yunchuan Liu, Abraham Canafe, Lei Yang
2023 IEEE Power & Energy Society General Meeting (PESGM) • 2023 21+ Citations | IEEE PESGM
📄 Official Publisher / DOI 🎓 Google Scholar Record

Abstract

Phasor Measurement Units (PMUs) stream mission-critical high-frequency voltage and current waveforms. We present a privacy-preserving federated deep learning framework allowing independent utility operators to train high-accuracy grid event classification networks without sharing proprietary telemetry, maintaining 88-90% accuracy across 3,877 real-world events.
