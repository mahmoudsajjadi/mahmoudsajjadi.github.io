Abstract

Standard Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA) scales linearly with model rank and weight matrix dimensions, incurring significant transmission costs during distributed edge adaptation and PCIe/VRAM bottlenecks in multi-tenant inference serving. SOLAR (Subspace-Oriented Latent Adapter Reparametrization) projects low-rank update matrices into shared orthogonal latent subspaces (\(\Delta W = P_U (B A) P_V^T\)), pruning redundant spectral directions prior to gradient synchronization. Across Vision Transformers (ViT), LLaMA-7B/13B, RoBERTa-Large, and GPT-2, SOLAR achieves a 35.1% reduction in memory footprint and transmission payloads while matching or outperforming standard LoRA (+0.3% average GLUE score) with zero custom CUDA kernels.
