SOLAR: Communication-Efficient Model Adaptation via Subspace-Oriented Latent Adapter Reparametrization

Seyed Mahmoud Sajjadi Mohammadabadi, Xiaolong Ma, Lei Yang, Feng Yan, Junshan Zhang
arXiv preprint arXiv:2604.08368 • 2026 35% Lower Memory than LoRA | Under Review ICML 2026
📄 arXiv Preprint 💻 Source Code / Artifact 🎓 Google Scholar Record

Abstract

Standard Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA) scales linearly with model rank and weight matrix dimensions, incurring significant transmission costs during distributed edge adaptation. SOLAR reparameterizes low-rank projections into orthogonal latent subspaces, achieving 35% smaller memory footprint and transmission payloads across Vision Transformers (ViT), LLaMA-3, and GPT-2 without degradation on downstream benchmarks.
