Smart Grid & Multi-Agent RL

Sustainable Smart Grid Resilience and Renewable Integration Using Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning

Seyed Mahmoud Sajjadi Mohammadabadi, Sayed Mohammad Sajjadi
Sustainability (MDPI, Vol. 18, Issue 20, 10574) • 2026 Open Access | +51% PV Hosting & 72.7% Fewer Evaluations
📄 Official Publisher (MDPI) 🔗 DOI: 10.3390/su182010574 📥 Full PDF 🎓 Google Scholar Profile

Abstract & Key Contributions

This work introduces a decentralized multi-agent reinforcement learning (MARL) framework designed to enable sustainable smart power grids to accommodate high levels of renewable energy generation while maintaining resilient operations under severe disruptions and multi-line faults. The architecture integrates autonomous feeder reconfiguration, Q-learning-based service restoration, coordinated battery energy storage dispatch, and communication-efficient decentralized training, validated under full AC power flow on the IEEE 33-bus feeder and the 179-bus Oberrhein benchmark network.

Key Quantitative Findings:
• Fault Service Restoration: Achieves identical performance to exhaustive centralized searches under single faults, and restores 55.1% of isolated load across all 496 double-fault line pairs while requiring 72.7% fewer feasibility evaluations.
• Renewable Hosting Capacity: Autonomous local Volt–VAr droop control increases photovoltaic (PV) hosting capacity by +51% over unity power factor without active power curtailment.
• Microgrid Islanding & Decarbonization: Coordinated storage dispatch raises fully served critical-load hours under localized islanding, avoiding 158–945 kg of CO₂ emissions per storm event.
