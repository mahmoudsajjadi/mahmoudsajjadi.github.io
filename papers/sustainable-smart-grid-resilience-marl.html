Sustainable Smart Grid Resilience and Renewable Integration Using Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning

Seyed Mahmoud Sajjadi Mohammadabadi, Sayed Mohammad Sajjadi
Sustainability (MDPI, Vol. 18, Issue 20, 10574) • 2026 Open Access | +51% PV Hosting & 72.7% Fewer Evaluations
📄 Official Publisher (MDPI) 🔗 DOI: 10.3390/su182010574 📥 Full PDF 🔬 ResearchGate 🎓 Google Scholar

Abstract & Key Contributions

Sustainable energy systems must accommodate renewable generation to ensure dependable service during severe disruptions. This paper presents a decentralized multi-agent system that integrates feeder reconfiguration, Q-learning-based restoration, coordinated battery dispatch, and communication-efficient training. AC power flow validates restoration and voltage-control decisions on the IEEE 33-bus feeder and the synthetic 179-bus Oberrhein benchmark network. For single faults, decentralized restoration achieves the same result as exhaustive centralized search. For double faults across all 496 possible line-fault pairs, it restores 55.1% of isolated load, against 56.6% for centralized search, with 72.7% fewer feasibility evaluations. The learned single-switch policy reaches 95.2% of the oracle reward within its six-action set. Workload balancing reduces the time to target performance by a factor of 1.46. In a localized islanding case, coordinated storage raises fully served critical-load hours from 0% to 4.2%. Autonomous local Volt–VAr droop control increases photovoltaic (PV) hosting capacity by 51% over unity power factor without active power curtailment. Restoration performance degrades gradually under communication loss. An illustrative calculation gives 158–945 kg of avoided CO₂ per storm event. All results come from simulation on benchmark networks.

Key Quantitative Findings:
• Sub-Second Bounded Decentralized Restoration: Matches exhaustive centralized search on single faults and restores 55.1% of isolated load across all 496 double-fault pairs with 72.7% fewer AC power-flow feasibility evaluations (61–114 ms bounded search satisfying 300–500 ms recloser dead times).
• Model-Free Q-Learning & ComDML Workload Balancing: Reaches 95.2% of oracle-optimal single-switch reward without power-flow model access, while ComDML workload balancing accelerates training convergence by 1.46×.
• Autonomous IEEE 1547-2018 Volt–VAr Hosting & Decarbonization: Increases PV hosting capacity by +51% over unity power factor with zero active power curtailment and avoids 158–945 kg CO₂ per storm event.
